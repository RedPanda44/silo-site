Good to know

License & project facts

What license is Silo released under?
MIT. You're free to use, modify and redistribute it — including for commercial purposes — provided the license notice is retained.
Is Silo affiliated with Apple?
No. Silo is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Apple Inc. Apple Pay, Apple Wallet and iOS are trademarks of Apple Inc.
Can I contribute?
Yes. This site lives at github.com/RedPanda44/silo-site — open a pull request or an issue there. The application source code itself is kept in the AirCard repository, where anyone can clone it and build it themselves.
Which languages are supported?
English and Simplified Chinese, switchable from the top bar. The selection is persisted locally for future launches. Additional translations are welcome.
Where do I report a problem?
On the GitHub issue tracker. Include your Windows version, iOS version, transport (USB/Wi‑Fi) and the exact step where things went sideways. Check the driver repair guide first — it fixes most reports.

Ready to try it?

One file, no install, fully reversible. See your new card face in under three minutes.
