Features

Two studios. One small app. Total control.

Silo packs a complete theming pipeline for iOS into a single native executable — from live card detection to reversible writes and a bilingual interface.

Wallet Studio

Custom card skins for Apple Pay & Apple Cash

Replace the artwork of any Wallet card with your own image — a texture, a photo, a bank logo, a piece of art. The studio handles cropping, scaling and writing for you.

  • PNG, JPG and WebP artwork support
  • Drag inside the preview to position the crop
  • Scales automatically to the exact 1536 × 969 card face
  • Local backup of the original face on first apply
  • Restore Original writes the backup back and invalidates Wallet's cache

Passcode Studio

.passthm lock-screen keypad themes

Import any .passthm package produced by Cowabunga or Nugget and push it to the iOS telephony cache. The built-in preview shows exactly how your keypad will look before you write anything.

  • Auto (TelephonyUI-10) for iOS 18+ — the default
  • TelephonyUI-9 for iOS 16 – 17
  • TelephonyUI-8 for legacy iOS
  • Realistic lock-screen preview with clock and key backplates
  • Works on both the lock screen and the Phone dialer

Under the hood

Built to be fast, safe and boring

No runtimes, no webviews, no telemetry. Just a native Rust binary that does one job well.

100% native & lightweight

A single standalone Silo.exe of roughly 7.5 MB. No Python, no Flet, no webview, no bloated runtimes.

Zero-hassle card detection

Tap any card in the iPhone's Wallet app while connected to detect its hash in real time through syslog_relay.

USB & Wi‑Fi transport

Scan card events and apply Wallet or passcode assets through USB or a paired local Wi‑Fi connection. Auto prefers USB, falls back to Wi‑Fi.

Snapshot & restore engine

A complete Books-state snapshot plus automatic restore preserves the original device state and every original card face.

Dashboard

Device status, saved cards, recent activity and one-click workflow shortcuts — everything visible at a glance on launch.

English / 简体中文 UI

Switch the interface language from the top bar. The selection is stored locally and restored on the next launch.

Workspace settings

Configure transport mode and interface language from a dedicated settings page, with an Appearance section for theme control.

Drag & drop everything

Drop artwork anywhere in the window to load it into the Wallet studio, or drop a .passthm package to open it in the Passcode studio.

Zero jailbreak

Utilizes Apple's built-in AirTraffic sync conduit without modifying system partitions or disabling security. Your device stays stock.

Interface

Designed like a native Apple tool

Built with egui and eframe, then themed to match the very Wallet it edits.

Light & dark

Instant switching from the top bar, remembered per machine.

Sidebar nav

macOS-style animated selection, app badge and device status card.

Hairline typography

Elevated cards, capsule buttons, separators and generous whitespace.

Micro-animations

Smooth transitions across every screen and focused UI modules.
