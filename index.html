What it does

Everything you need to theme your iPhone.

Two focused studios, a live dashboard, and a transport layer that talks to your iPhone over USB or paired Wi‑Fi.

Custom Card Skins

Assign custom artwork, textures or bank logos to your Apple Pay and Apple Cash cards — with drag-to-crop and exact 1536 × 969 scaling.

Passcode Themes (.passthm)

Apply popular Cowabunga & Nugget .passthm keypad themes straight to the iOS lock screen and Phone dialer.

Safe & Reversible

A full Books-state snapshot and automatic restore engine back up the original card face before anything is replaced.

USB & Wi‑Fi Transport

Scan card events and push assets over USB or a paired local network. Auto prefers USB and falls back to Wi‑Fi automatically.

Zero Jailbreak

No system partitions are modified and no security is disabled — Silo rides Apple's built-in AirTraffic sync conduit.

Dashboard & Live Detection

Device status, saved cards, recent activity and one-click shortcuts. Tap a card in Wallet and its hash is intercepted in real time via syslog_relay.

How it works

Three minutes from download to a new card.

Install & connect

Grab Silo.exe, plug in your iPhone with a Lightning or USB‑C cable, unlock it and tap Trust this Computer.

Scan the card

Stay on the Wallet tab, click Scan, then tap the card in your iPhone's Wallet app. Silo intercepts and stores the hash — click Stop.

Apply the skin

Choose your artwork, position the crop, click Apply Card Skin, then force-close and reopen Wallet on the iPhone to see it.

iPhone not detected? Corrupted or conflicting Apple USB drivers on Windows are the number-one root cause. Install 3uTools, disconnect the iPhone, run Toolbox → Repair Driver → Repair Now, then reconnect and tap Trust. Full walkthrough in the troubleshooting guide.
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone?
No. Silo uses Apple's built-in AirTraffic sync conduit and never modifies system partitions or disables security. Your device stays stock and updatable.
Which iOS versions are supported?
iOS 18 and newer are fully supported (TelephonyUI-10 cache). iOS 16–17 themes are supported through the TelephonyUI-9 target, and there's a legacy TelephonyUI-8 option for older devices.
Is it safe? Can I undo a change?
Yes. Before the first apply, Silo takes a complete Books-state snapshot and backs up the original Wallet card face locally. Restore Original writes it back and invalidates Wallet's cached artwork.
Why doesn't my passcode theme show up?
Turn Bold Text off in Settings → Display & Brightness. When Bold Text is enabled, iOS ignores cached dialer button graphics and renders system vector fonts instead. See the passcode guide.
Can I work without a USB cable?
Yes. Pair once over USB, enable "Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi" in Apple Devices or iTunes, and keep both devices on the same local network. Silo's Auto transport prefers USB and falls back to Wi‑Fi; choose Wi‑Fi only for a guaranteed wireless route.
How big is it, and does it need anything installed?
A single ~7.7 MB Silo.exe. No Python, no Flet, no webview, no bundled runtimes. The only external requirement is Apple Mobile Device Support (64-bit iTunes) for talking to your iPhone.
